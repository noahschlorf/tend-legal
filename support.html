Common Requests

  • Missing course: Use the in-app course request flow, or email the course name, city, state, tee, and a scorecard photo.
  • Course data correction: Email the course name, tee, hole number, and the corrected par, yardage, or handicap.
  • Restore purchase: Open the paywall and tap Restore Purchases.
  • Cancel subscription: Open App Store account settings, then Subscriptions, then Tend.
  • Refunds: All purchases are billed by Apple. Request refunds at reportaproblem.apple.com.
  • Delete account: Open Tend, go to Profile, then Delete Account. This removes server-side records and the Supabase Auth account.
  • Microphone not working: Confirm microphone permission is enabled in iOS Settings, Tend, Microphone.
  • Voice not transcribing: Cloud voice requires a network connection and Tend Pro. Manual logging works offline and is free.
